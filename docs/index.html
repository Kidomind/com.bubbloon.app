Bubbloon – Privacy Policy

Bubbloon is a bubble-popping game that requires no account. We do not collect personal data such as name, email or location. Progress, scores, coins and language are stored only on your device.

The game shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process advertising ID and device/usage data under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). A one-time "Remove ads" purchase disables ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
